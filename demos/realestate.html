Why invest in Dubai

A city built for ambition — and for living well.

International buyers are drawn to Dubai for its tax environment, residency pathways and lifestyle. A few of the fundamentals our advisors walk clients through:

0%

Personal income tax

No personal income tax on salaries in the UAE — a key consideration for relocating professionals and families.

10yr

Residency pathways

Long-term residence visas are available to qualifying property investors, subject to current government criteria.

100%

Freehold ownership

Foreign nationals may own property outright in designated freehold areas, including all four districts we cover.

GMT+4

A global crossroads

A time-zone bridge between Europe, Asia and Africa, with direct flights to major cities worldwide.

Illustrative overview for a concept website only. Regulations change; this is not legal, tax or investment advice. Always verify with qualified professionals.

Clear, regulated transactions

Escrow-backed off-plan purchases and a transparent registration process.

World-class infrastructure

Modern transport, private schools, healthcare and leisure on the doorstep.

Year-round lifestyle

Beaches, marinas, golf and a dining scene that rivals any global capital.
